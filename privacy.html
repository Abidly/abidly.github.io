Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short

  • Abidly holds sensitive information about your recovery, so we collect only what the app needs to work, and we treat all of it as private.
  • We never sell your information, never show ads, and never use it for marketing or to train AI.
  • Only you can see what you track. Accountability partners see only what you choose to share with them.
  • Your Faith Journal is encrypted on your phone. We can’t read it, and we can’t reset your journal passphrase.
  • You can download your data or delete your account, and everything in it, at any time in the app.

Who we are

Abidly is operated by Phoebe Acevedo, a sole trader based in Queensland, Australia (ABN 25 651 073 876) (“Abidly”, “we”, “us”). We are responsible for your personal information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Where other privacy laws apply to you, such as the GDPR in Europe and the UK, we follow them as well.

This policy covers the Abidly app for iPhone and Android and this website.

What we collect

Some of what you tell Abidly is sensitive information under Australian law, such as your health and your recovery from addiction. We collect it only with your consent, which you give when you create an account, and only so the app can do what you ask it to.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don’t collect your contacts, photos, precise location history or browsing.

What stays on your phone

  • Location: only when you ask to find help near you. Your phone works out your country or area once; we don’t store your location.
  • Camera clips: optional 10-second clips of an activity are saved on your phone only and are never uploaded.
  • Steps: counted only during a walk you start.
  • Speech to text: where your phone supports it, your voice is turned into text on the phone. If it can’t, Abidly asks before using Apple’s or Google’s speech service. Journal audio is never recorded or saved.
  • Your journal key, Face ID setting, reminders and celebrations are kept on your phone.
  • The Journey Book and journal PDFs are made on your phone. We don’t keep a copy.

How we use it

We use your information only to run Abidly for you: to show your progress, rings and patterns; to connect you with partners and send alerts; to provide Premium; to keep the service secure; and to answer you when you contact us. We don’t use it to make decisions about you, to profile you for advertising, or to train artificial intelligence.

Who can see it

  • You. Everything you track is private to you by default.
  • Your accountability partners see only what you share with them: prayer requests and reach-out messages always, and milestones, fresh starts or check-ins only if you turn them on. They never see your journal, rescues, urge scores, patterns or other trackers. You can end a partnership at any time.
  • Us. We access your information only when needed to run, fix or secure the service, or when you ask us for help.
  • Service providers listed below, only to provide their part of Abidly.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell, rent or trade your information.

Where it’s stored

Your Abidly account data is stored in Australia (Sydney). Some information is handled overseas by the providers above, mainly in the United States: your push token and the content of push alerts (Expo, Apple, Google), and your subscription status and anonymous account ID (RevenueCat). We choose providers with strong security and privacy commitments and limit what they receive.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: for as long as you have an account.
  • Things shared with partners (prayer requests, messages, voice messages and updates): deleted automatically after 60 days. You can delete a prayer request sooner.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your account and everything in it straight away, including voice messages, and remove your record from RevenueCat. Copies may remain in our hosting provider’s automatic backups for a short time before they are overwritten.
  • Help suggestions you sent are kept without your name after you delete your account.

How we protect it

  • Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS), and data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Database rules make sure each person can reach only their own information, and partners only what’s shared with them.
  • Your Faith Journal is encrypted on your phone with a key protected by your passphrase. We can’t read it or reset the passphrase, so keep your recovery code safe.
  • You can lock your journal with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Your data → Download my data.
  • Correct it: change it in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete it: delete single items in the app, or Profile → Your data → Delete my account.
  • Stop sharing: change what each partner sees, or end a partnership, at any time.
  • Turn off alerts and permissions in the app or your phone’s settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict how we use your information. Contact us and we’ll respond within 30 days.

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, or to the privacy regulator where you live.

Adults only

Abidly is for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
