Support

How can we help?

Need help right now? If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number. In Australia, call 000, or Lifeline on 13 11 14. In the app, tap “Need help right now?” or “Urgent help” for crisis lines in your country.
How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Abidly. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Abidly. You can also tap “Manage subscription” on Abidly’s Premium screen.

I forgot my journal passphrase

Open the journal and tap “Use my recovery code instead”. For your privacy, Abidly can’t see or reset your passphrase. If you’ve lost both the passphrase and the recovery code, the old entries can’t be opened, but you can start a fresh journal.

How do I invite an accountability partner?

Profile → Accountability partners → Invite. Send them the code. They download Abidly, tap “I’m here to support someone”, and enter it. Being a partner is free.

What can my partner see?

Only what you share: prayer requests and reach-out messages, plus milestones, fresh starts or check-ins if you turn those on for that partner. Never your journal, rescues or patterns.

How do I download my data?

Profile → Your data → Download my data. You’ll get a file with everything Abidly holds about you.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Your data → Delete my account. It deletes your account and everything in it, permanently. If you have Premium, please cancel the subscription first, because deleting your account doesn’t cancel it.

I’m not getting prayer alerts

Check that notifications are allowed for Abidly in your phone’s settings, and that “Prayer alerts” is turned on in Abidly under Accountability partners.

Email abidly@getverseflow.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within a few days. For privacy questions, email abidly@getverseflow.com.
