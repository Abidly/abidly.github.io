What’s inside

Everything you need on the hard days, and the good ones

Track your growth

Watch your sprout grow with every day. A setback never erases how far you’ve come; it’s simply a fresh start.

Rescue when it’s hard

“I’m struggling” leads to prayer, Scripture, a short walk or activity, breathing, or a calm 3-minute game to ride out the urge.

Accountability partners

Invite up to three people you trust. Ask for prayer or reach out with a message or voice note, and their phone chimes straight away.

Daily devotionals

Short readings from the Bible, with a reflection, something to try and a prayer.

A private Faith Journal

Write or speak your prayers. Entries are encrypted on your phone, so not even Abidly can read them.

Help near you

Crisis lines for your country, plus counsellors, support groups and Christian organisations where you live.

Our promise

Your journey stays yours

Built to help you grow, not to keep you here. After five years of freedom, you graduate, and you’re invited to walk with someone else.

  • No ads, ever. We never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • Only you can see what you track. Partners see only what you choose to share.
  • Your journal is end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase only you know.
  • Download everything, or delete your account and all of it, any time from the app.
  • Daily check-ins, rescue tools, partners and urgent help are always free.
